AI adoption, done in the open

One front door for AI ideas.

Request, route, approve, measure.

Who it's for: operations, enablement and team leads at a growing company who get more AI ideas than they can check, and want each one routed, risk-checked and measured.

People submit an idea in plain words. The front desk asks for what's missing, scores value, effort, data sensitivity and risk, spots duplicates, and suggests an existing tool when one fits. A person approves. The adoption kit trains the team, and the dashboard shows whether it helped.

Ideas pile up

Every team has a list of "could AI do this?" ideas in chats, emails and docs, with no single place to send them.

They overlap

    Safety and governance

    A person approves

    Nothing is built, connected or rolled out without a recorded human decision and reason.

    Outside text is data

    Requests are read, never obeyed. In AI mode, Claude sees only redacted text and can raise a risk, never lower it.

    Nothing extra stored

    The demo keeps nothing. Logs hold counts only, and decisions record a role, not a name.

    Redaction first

    Bank details, cards, emails, phones and keys are removed before a request is shown, compared or sent anywhere.

    No HR data, no decisions about people

    Hiring, pay, performance and monitoring requests go to human review. The front desk never connects to HR systems.

    GDPR in mind

    Only what the task needs, with a clear purpose. Call recordings need consent, and personal data stays in approved tools.
