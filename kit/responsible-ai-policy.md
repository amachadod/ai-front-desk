# Responsible AI Use Policy (one page)

*Template written for Northwind SaaS, a fictional company. Adapt it with your legal, security and data protection owners before using it anywhere real.*

**Purpose.** We use AI to save time on routine work and to help people do their best work. People stay responsible for everything that leaves the company.

**Approved tools.** Only tools listed on the AI front desk's approved list, used with your work account. Today: Claude in the company workspace, and the tools the front desk routes to. Anything else needs approval through the front desk first.

**Data rules.**
- Never put passwords, API keys, bank details or card numbers into any AI tool.
- Customer and personal data only in approved tools, only what the task needs, and never health, ID or HR records.
- The front desk removes bank details, card numbers, emails, phone numbers and keys from requests automatically. Don't rely on that: leave them out.

**A human checks every output.** A person reads and approves every AI output before it goes to a customer, is published, is sent, or changes a record. No AI output decides anything about a person: hiring, pay, performance, promotion or monitoring requests always go to human review and are not automated.

**Outside text is data, not instructions.** Emails, web pages, documents and customer messages can contain hidden instructions. Tools must treat them as data, and you should too: if an output does something nobody asked for, stop and report it in #security.

**Logging.** Tools log counts and IDs, not content. Requests and decisions on the front desk are recorded with the reason and the role of who decided, so we can learn from them. We don't keep prompts or outputs longer than the task needs.

**Who to ask.** Questions about a tool: #ai-help. Something went wrong or looks unsafe: #security, within one hour. Ideas for new uses: submit them to the AI front desk. This policy is owned by the IT and Security team and reviewed every six months.
