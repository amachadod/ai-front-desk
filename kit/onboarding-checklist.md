# One-week checklist: your first week with an approved AI tool

For anyone at Northwind SaaS (a fictional company) who has just been given access to a tool through the AI front desk. Tick each line, and ask in #ai-help if anything is unclear.

## Day 1
- [ ] Read the one-page [Responsible AI Use Policy](responsible-ai-policy.md).
- [ ] Sign in to the approved tool with your work account and two-factor authentication.
- [ ] Find the three prompts for your team in the [prompt library](prompt-library.md).

## Day 2
- [ ] Use the tool on one real task that has no customer or personal data in it.
- [ ] Do the "check before you send" step and note what you changed.

## Day 3
- [ ] Join the 40-minute training session (or watch the recording).
- [ ] Ask one question you had in #ai-help.

## Day 4
- [ ] Use the tool on two more real tasks.
- [ ] Rate each output (accepted as is, edited, rejected) in the tool's feedback button.

## Day 5
- [ ] Answer the two-question pulse survey.
- [ ] If you have a new idea, submit it to the AI front desk with one example and a time estimate.
- [ ] Tell your manager one thing that saved time and one thing that didn't work.
